# ADR-011: Research Compression Metrics

## Status
Accepted

## Context
Scientific tooling often reports subjective "quality scores". Scientific value cannot be reduced to a single scalar quality score.

## Decision
Measure research compression via process efficiency and completeness metrics (`ResearchCompressionMetrics`): papers screened vs inspected, automated screening ratio, hypotheses eliminated, physical experiments avoided, provenance coverage, and reproducibility score.

## Consequences
- Transparent, factual benchmarking of computational acceleration without pseudo-scientific quality claims.
